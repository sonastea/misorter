# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People creating and ranking ordered lists, including anonymous visitors and people
opening shared lists.

## Product Purpose

Help people build a list and rank its items through comparisons. Import and export
let people move their input lists without requiring an account or a share link.

## Brand Personality

Straightforward, friendly, compact. Preserve the established Misorter interface.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style UI and visual redesigns within existing product workflows.

## Design Principles

- Keep the next action clear.
- Preserve user content and make errors repairable.
- Keep local draft work independent of sharing and persistence.
- Follow existing components and light/dark themes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide accessible control names, announced errors, keyboard navigation, managed
dialog focus, and mobile-friendly bounded previews.
